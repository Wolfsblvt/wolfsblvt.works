# Yoru: character sheet

## Meaning

Who Yoru is and what must not change when he is drawn again. Every new pose and animation starts here; the sprite
studio puts the identity lock below into every generation prompt. The canonical image is
[`reference/canonical-base.png`](reference/canonical-base.png), the base Codex generated on 2026-05-29 that every one
of his original animation rows was drawn from. Where this text and that image disagree, the image wins.

## Identity lock

The block between the markers is read by `tools/yoru-studio` and sent with every generation. Keep it a description
of what is visible, not of mood.

Yoru is a small nocturnal familiar, not a wolf, dog or ordinary cat. Charcoal-black fur, compact body, usually upright

lower leg. A long thin tail ending in a copper-to-blue feathery tuft, with a gold ring near its base. Small dark claws.
Painterly anime-fantasy rendering with dark outlines, soft fur texture and a restrained palette: charcoal, deep plum,

Details that carry the identity at 96 × 104 on the site, in order: the ear silhouette, the red-to-blue forelock, the
plum capelet with its gold edge, the tail tuft, the blue pendant. Constellation lines and leg marks may soften at that
size; they may not change shape or move.

He is asymmetric. The forelock falls over one side of his face and does not swap sides when he turns; a left-facing
pose is drawn, not mirrored, unless the mirror has been checked frame by frame.

## Personality

Wolf, 2026-10-24: aloof with earned mischief, and "it leans in that direction from time to time about being curious
and sleepy, or trying to poke and touch stuff himself."

- **Self-possessed.** He watches before he joins. He is not performing for the visitor and does not need their
  approval; he is visibly unimpressed more often than he is excited.
- **Curious and sleepy.** Things catch his attention: a light, an edge, something that moved. He goes and touches them
  himself. Between those moments he is happy to rest, and he sleeps properly, curled up.
- **Mischief is rare and earned.** A deliberate, slightly unnecessary effort (fetching a ladder for a modest ledge),
  nudging something off an edge to see what happens, a smug look back. Rare enough that it lands.
- **Quietly loyal.** He comes along when you move through the site, at his own pace, and sometimes stays behind.

His face carries most of it: half-lidded eyes, ears, a slight head tilt. Big gestures, pleading, distress and cartoon
emotion symbols are not him.

## Acting rules for animation

- Weight is real: feet stay planted on a ledge, a landing compresses, a curl lowers the body before it rounds.
- The capelet and tail follow the body a beat late; they make most of the secondary motion.
- Transitions are authored in both directions. Waking is not curling played backwards.
- No detached effects: no ZZZ, sparkles, motion lines, sweat drops, floor shadows. Everything that moves is part of him
  or a prop he holds.
