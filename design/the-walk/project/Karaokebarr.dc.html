Karaokebarr, in its window

Karaokebarr already has a complete brand: the labeled-disc mark, Ink and Paper with Apricot, Mint and Lilac, Bricolage Grotesque and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, and a Disc with a real hole. Nothing provisional needed inventing. This board recovers that brand and puts it in the product’s window on the Works walk, where the Disc does what the product promises: personal music becoming ready to sing. Nothing here is integrated or published.

01 The brand, recovered

Source: karaokebarr/docs/design/brand/BRAND.md, the tokens in docs/design/tokens/ and the UI guide. Fixed there: dark only, no mascot ever, Ink and Paper as the base, Apricot for the one primary action, Mint for editorial, Lilac only on Disc labels. The mark here is the repository’s karaokebarr-mark.svg, copied byte for byte into design/the-walk/assets/ with its content credentials intact.

02 The window

Arr outside, disc inside, exactly as the guide says: Ink and hairlines for the window, colour only on the Disc and the one Apricot action. Mint eyebrow, lockup with the mark at cap height, the tagline as the headline. Standing is a word in a slate badge before anything else. On the right the Disc arrives as a draft (dashed cut-guide, desaturated), becomes saved (plain, Mint), and burns (the Apricot ring drawn once, lock badge). That is the product’s promise shown on its own object, and it is the only motion the guide allows a Disc besides the 4° hover.

04 What this decided, and what it didn’t

Decided by the design (reversible)

  • No provisional wordmark or theme: the existing brand is complete and is used as-is. Inventing a lighter stand-in would have been a second design system.
  • The Disc, not a screenshot, is the window’s object. Its lifecycle is the motion, drawn once, within the guide’s own motion list.
  • The window keeps Ink inside the Works plum. The two darks are different enough to read as a change of room.
  • Standing is a word, before the promise: “Designed · not yet built”.

Still open

  • Copy belongs to W-B; the body text here is the brand pitch shortened and is marked provisional. The Disc title is fictional.
  • Fonts: production self-hosts both faces. Fontsource carries @fontsource-variable/bricolage-grotesque and @fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible-next (both 5.3.0, OFL); that is two packages for the site’s font step, not yet added.
  • Asset rights: the mark and brand are Wolf’s own, made for Karaokebarr; the copied SVG keeps its content credentials. Nothing from the player or any catalogue is used.
  • Implementation standing: nothing integrated. There is no Karaokebarr.astro window yet; this would be a new section in the Walk’s order, which Nyxara integrates. The window CSS maps onto src/styles/home.css the way diffdevil’s does.
