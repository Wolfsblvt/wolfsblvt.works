The devil is in the diff.

Measure changes. Match rules. Act on the result.

Explore diffdevil →
$ diffdevil analyze
diffdevil analysis
Changed +128 −42 ~19
Measurement status: exact
Band: size/M

Git gives you a patch and a pair of raw counters. diffdevil turns the details inside that diff into facts your scripts and workflows can inspect, query, evaluate, and act on.

I care what we make.And why.

even if I ramble a lot

I want to make things people can use, get attached to, or get curious about. A daily annoyance is a reason to care. So is the possibility of an experience that doesn’t exist yet.

There’s a lot of thinking behind that. We ramble, dream, work through details and make plans. We keep exploring what the thing could be while we build it, put it into people’s hands, fix what isn’t working, and rethink what comes next.

I have opinions about how things should work. We keep the reasons behind those choices with the work, so a new version doesn’t quietly forget what mattered. Some decisions hold up. Others change. I want us to understand why.

Shikkuri

An evening to play. Not another evening choosing.

A library full of games can leave you scrolling until the evening is gone. Shikkuri is an idea for finding games that fit you and the time you actually have.

A larger project, currently paused.

How it started

It started with a much smaller question: “Can I let an agent read my steam library and the categories I assigned games to inside Steam?”

That grew into a detailed game-intelligence concept. I paused it when I realised how much attention doing it properly would take, and that starting with smaller projects might be the better way in.

Lunescence

An imagined moment.

You say something unexpected. Before the reply, there’s a glance, a change of expression, a small gesture you start to recognise.

Lunescence is my idea for a 3D AI companion: expressive, recognisable, and full of small particularities. I want to explore what happens when its words, movements and personality belong to the same character.

Early concept. Currently paused.
