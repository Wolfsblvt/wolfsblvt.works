The Pile · pieces v2

Eight different objects, not eight kinds of card.

Each kind has its own silhouette and material: bare type, a paper slip, an app icon, a little terminal, two stickers, a door, the toy itself, a ticket. Colour stays inside the Smoke & Fireworks family (bone, rose, cyan, ember, plum); only projects with their own colour bring it, and mostly in Endless. The pile engine still knows none of this: it measures, places, drops and lifts.
