smolpng, in its window

A light-first candidate for smolpng’s identity, shown where it will first be met: its window on the Works walk. Two versions of the same window, each at desktop and phone width. Wolf picks by letter, or mixes. The palette is leaned on Gura’s official colours, as asked; Squishling is here in ASCII until the real drawing exists. Nothing here is integrated or published.

01 The palette, light-first

Sampled from the official full-body renders on 2026-10-22 (hoodie, hood shadow, mouth, skin, white, hair greys) and checked against the community palette the dark-first Gura Sparks sheet was derived from. Contrast on paper: ink 12.4:1, marine 5.3:1, coral 5.1:1, quiet ink 4.6:1. Avoid, as the dark sheet already says: all five sparks at equal weight, cyan glow on controls, shark motifs, pure white. Keep coral present enough that this never becomes another calm blue tool page.

05 What this decided, and what it didn’t

Decided by the design (reversible)

  • smolpng’s window is light inside the dark-only site. The Vision says projects speak in their own colour inside their window; a light window is the strongest form of that, and it needs a line in Decisions when integrated.
  • The mascot is the main thing: Squishling’s full drawing, large, in a reserved shape, at every width.
  • Drag-and-drop is shown, not offered: an animated illustration with a finished state, real numbers, marked illustrative.
  • Standing is a word before the headline. No fake finished mascot, no fake website.

Still open

  • Wolf: A or B, or A’s paper with B’s type. Whether smolpng gets its own typeface at all.
  • Copy belongs to W-A. Every sentence here is provisional and marked.
  • Squishling’s rights: the commissioned drawing does not exist; the ASCII is Wolf’s. Artist credit lands with the art.
  • Gura’s colours are leaned on, not licensed: the sheet carries hex values only, no artwork, no character.
  • Implementation standing: nothing integrated. Production reads src/sections/projects/Smolpng.astro and src/styles/home.css; the window CSS here maps onto them one to one, and Nunito (OFL) would be one new Fontsource package if B’s type is chosen.
