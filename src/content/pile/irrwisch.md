An Irrwisch is a will-o’-the-wisp, and here that’s you: a figure with a lantern walks into a clearing in the woods, and a window lights up. Then you can build one room, leave one small thing, chalk a few cells on a slate, or shoo one of the one-eyed little ones a step along. Nobody can tear anything down. The place is whatever everyone made of it.
