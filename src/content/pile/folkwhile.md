Folkwhile is a single-player village game that lives at the edge of your desktop. Get to know its people, support their ideas, and help a small settlement grow into a place with a history. Choose what to build, make room for new skills, and follow the relationships that change along the way. Work, friendship and ordinary pleasures carry on while you’re away.
