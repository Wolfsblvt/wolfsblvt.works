# The Pile: authoring objects and Endless windows

## Meaning

The Pile is the changing, compact encounter with a deliberately chosen public collection. Endless is the same collection given a stable order and individually authored windows into its subjects. An entry has one lasting identity, one compact preview, and one expanded presentation. Its content and presentation can grow without losing its address.

This is the reusable authoring guide for Wolfsblvt Works and future sessions adding or improving entries. It carries Wolf's selected direction, the eight-object repertoire, writing guidance, useful expansion, links, and the authoring path. It replaces the earlier guide, including its now-superseded uncertainty about emptying the Pile. It does not reopen the Works voice or certify the availability of a pictured product.

Read [the experience specification](pile-experience.md) for empty-state copy, choreography, anchors, navigation, and closing. Read [the technical plan](pile-technical-plan.md) for files, types, rendering, daily selection, custom components, and verification. The product decisions come from Wolf's co-design; editorial ranges and concrete implementation choices are Nyxara-authored guidance. This is a completed source return, not a claim that the website has been implemented.

## The editorial centre

**The compact object catches attention. Its Endless window rewards it.**

Endless must not merely append more entries or enlarge the same icon. Where useful public material exists, give the visitor a little more: orientation, a short explanation, an aside, an image, a meaningful relationship, a useful example, a link, or the thing itself.

The reward is not measured in paragraphs. A deliberately quiet door, a complete one-line thought, or a beautiful visual fragment can be sufficient. Do not pad a private subject or invent facts to fill its space.

The Pile is not a backlog, product ranking, public roadmap vote, or collection of ideas awaiting permission to matter. Small public presentations can conceal substantial work. Some questions stay questions. Finished things, experiments, personal interests, and ambitious projects can belong together.

Works supplies the surrounding voice. An established product can bring its own language and branding into its window. Do not manufacture a separate brand for every entry.

## One identity, two presentations

Every compact object is one genuine link to its own Endless entry. This rule includes notes, apparent terminal links, ticket stubs, dice, and closed doors. There are no outbound destinations, playable controls, or inert decorative non-actions inside compact previews.

All destination links and actual controls belong in Endless. The compact die invites opening its window, not rolling; the compact ticket invites seeing the destination portrait, not leaving the site.

Preserve recognition between the two views through the title, material, silhouette, asset, or another strong identifying detail. An expanded app window need not remain icon-shaped, but it should plainly belong to the icon that opened it.

An entry may gain a website, documentation, screenshot, branding, or custom specimen later. Its ID and permalink do not change. Switching its most fitting physical form also does not require a new identity.

Endless order is editorial and independent of the date. The daily Pile's selected assortment and placement change. Both refer to the same entries, including entries that are not present in today's assortment.

## Writing vocabulary

These are soft starting ranges, not validation limits, mandatory fields, or a requirement to fill every slot.

| Element | Job | Starting guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title or main statement | Identify the subject or present the actual thought. | A real name, question, or sentence. Do not truncate it for symmetry. |
| Sub-line | Orient the reader quickly. | About 6–18 words: what it is, what it does, who it is for, or its unusual premise. |
| Explanatory paragraph | Add useful understanding. | Usually 30–75 words and one paragraph. A second short paragraph is justified only by a distinct useful thought. |
| Wolf scribble | Add a personal observation, preference, reservation, joke, or sketch. | Often 3–14 words, or one small drawing. Optional. |
| Standing | Prevent a real misunderstanding about readiness. | One plain phrase, at most once per entry, when needed. Examples: “Concept”, “In development”, “Private project”. Not a caption under the visual. |
| Visual or example | Show something more effectively than another paragraph. | Usually one focal addition: screenshot, image, SVG, small chart, snippet, brand asset, or specimen. |
| Cross-links | Offer meaningful onward routes. | Existing destinations only; as many as are useful, without a quota or empty placeholders. |

**Sub-line is orientation. Paragraph is understanding. Scribble is an additional personal thought.** Do not paraphrase the same information three times in different fonts.

“Short” does not mean cryptic. Complete sentences normally beat compressed product jargon. A paragraph should not require the reader to understand the internal project history.

A scribble is not compulsory comedy. Sincerity can stand. Proposed first-person wording is authored copy for Wolf's acceptance, not a fabricated quotation or an invented memory.

Use “I” for Wolf's own perspective and “we” for actual shared work. An origin story, a mechanism, a possible experience, and a present-day useful outcome are different ways to introduce something; select the one that makes this object interesting.

Do not infer availability, release dates, commercial terms, technical guarantees, or personal disclosures from a mockup.

### A small writing comparison

The following is an illustrative editorial specimen, not a real product announcement.

**Title:** A notebook that stays a notebook.

**Sub-line:** Notes without the second-brain project.

**Paragraph:** I want somewhere to put a thought without first deciding how it fits into a system. Write it down, find it again, and leave it alone when it no longer matters. The interesting part is how much structure helps before it starts asking for work of its own.

**Possible scribble:** preferably not another hobby to maintain.

These pieces add different information. An actual entry might use the title and paragraph, or the title and scribble, rather than all four.

## The eight physical forms

These are a shared repertoire, not a closed taxonomy or eight uniform information schemas. Their names are authoring shorthand, not required public category labels. The supplied object reference owns the visual starting point; the rules below intentionally change its compact interactions and enrich its expanded presentations.

### Thought: bare words

**Compact:** The thought itself, set directly on the Pile. A question-mark treatment or underline can support it.

**Endless:** Keep the thought prominent. Add a sub-line, short explanatory paragraph, next thought, chosen connection, scribble, or small visual when that gives the visitor something useful.

A question does not have to grow into a product, and every thought does not need a timeline of its origin. Some are complete statements.

### Note: paper and tape

**Compact:** A slip of paper with tape, with an authored handwritten or typewritten treatment.

**Endless:** Keep the paper and tape. Allow a sub-line, margin scribble, sketch, or a little additional writing. Do not replace the object with a generic content panel.

Use about four curated variants rather than one silhouette copied everywhere or a procedural stationery generator:

- a short, slightly crooked slip;
- a wider, lightly rotated note;
- a taller torn sheet;
- a folded-corner or differently torn note.

Vary tape length, angle, paper proportions, and the two typographic treatments within this small authored set. A chosen variant belongs to the entry; daily positioning may vary without rerolling its whole personality. Expanded notes keep a readable type size rather than scaling a short sentence into a giant poster.

### Chip: icon-sized software

**Compact:** An icon, image, or mark with enough title/platform information to recognise the subject.

**Endless:** An application-like glimpse, not merely a larger icon. A sub-line and paragraph can sit beside a real screenshot, image, feature example, or branding asset. Existing installation, source, app, and documentation links fit naturally nearby.

A real icon or screenshot is allowed to be the visual fragment. No separate image category is required. Label interface fixtures as fixtures rather than suggesting an unbuilt product already works.

### Artifact: the useful thing itself

**Compact:** A believable snippet of command line, output, code, or another small useful artifact.

**Endless:** Extend the same material. A few comments can carry orientation and explanation; an input/output pair or actual invocation may be the most useful expansion. Keep code selectable and provide a copy control only for an example worth copying.

Render destinations as real links, not fake executable commands. A terminal can have a small footer or edge strip reading “Source · Documentation”. Explanatory comments can stay inside the terminal; navigation should remain recognisable as navigation.

Do not treat the reference's example command as verified software behavior.

### Lineage: a relationship made visible

**Compact:** Marks, tags, stickers, or nodes connected by an expressive arrow. The relationship is the object; credit belongs in the picture when credit is due.

**Endless:** Give the relationship room. Add a sub-line, a short explanation of what changed or followed, a scribble, and appropriate links beside the things they belong to.

This is broader than an open-source fork. It can show another of Wolf's tools being extended, an existing tool receiving an addition, an idea developing into another, or a combination of useful work. Karaokebarr was raised as a possible fit to explore, not as a verified dependency statement to publish.

Use accurate relationship language. “Forked from”, “builds on”, “inspired by”, “extends”, and “became” are different claims. Do not draw technical dependency where only conceptual inspiration is known. Do not imply endorsement.

A source, its descendant, and the connection can each remain intelligible without becoming a knowledge-graph application.

### Door: a deliberate boundary

**Compact:** A closed door with its name, warm detail, and short line.

**Endless:** A quietly composed door with the same public boundary can be complete. Extra space does not purchase extra disclosure.

A single door is the default starting composition. A small group of three or four is available only when several genuine subjects benefit from sharing the scene. Grouping is not mandatory, and no other unrelated windows should be packed together just to fill the page.

If doors are grouped, each retains its own title, stable anchor, and identifiable target region. The group's composition does not replace its members' identities.

The compact door opens its Endless window. The expanded door is not an unlockable promise. Baumhaus retains the selected limited disclosure unless Wolf separately changes it.

### Specimen: a small experience

**Compact:** A recognisable still, fixture, small visual, or representation of the thing. It opens Endless; it does not play the control.

**Endless:** The thing itself when useful: an actual control, animation, tiny simulation, visual experiment, or honestly labelled static fixture.

A specimen need not be interactive to belong. An entry may start with ordinary text or a static render and later gain an authored component under the same ID. Its future ambition should not force placeholder machinery today.

Real controls need real states and honest behavior. A local demonstration is not evidence of a live product integration. Do not silently turn the website into a credentialed app.

### Site ticket: a place to visit

**Compact:** A ticket showing the destination's identity or domain. The whole ticket opens its Endless window.

**Endless:** A more developed ticket or destination portrait. The actual target site's theme and appropriate assets can shape it. Add a useful line, short paragraph, preview, scribble, or related destinations where they help.

The outbound stub is a real link. For ordinary same-tab activation, it tears away before navigation. Modified clicks, explicit new-tab behavior, reduced motion, or a failed animation must still produce a working ordinary link. The experience and technical documents define that join.

Do not fetch arbitrary site previews live or imply ownership of a third-party destination. Use intentional, correctly attributed assets.

## Cross-links are content, not decoration

Links are available to every form, including an otherwise minimal door when its actual public boundary permits one. Omit them when no destination exists.

Useful meanings include source repository, own website, app/dashboard, documentation, community/Discord, download, original/upstream, and another Endless entry.

Store the meaning, readable label, and destination. Select its icon separately. “GitHub source”, “Open dashboard”, “Read the docs”, and “Join the Discord” are distinguishable actions. Multiple similar destinations need labels rather than a puzzle made of icons.

Icon-only links may fit a small window, provided they retain a proper accessible name and a label on hover and keyboard focus. On touch, the action must remain intelligible without a required hover. Where ambiguity remains, keep the visible text.

Links need not occupy one repeated footer. They can sit beside lineage nodes, beneath an artifact, at the edge of a note, next to an app image, or on a ticket stub. Reuse the semantic link component without imposing its layout.

A source link is not automatically an installation recommendation. A public repository can be unfinished. An upstream link is not the same as the entry's own source.

Add a newly available website or docs link by editing the existing entry, not creating a replacement entry or a “coming soon” button in advance. Related-entry links address stable IDs, never daily positions.

## Branding, media, and public truth

Use the actual assets available and appropriate to the subject. A visual can be a sketch, screenshot, branded surface, SVG, small chart, or specimen inside one of the existing forms. A ninth category is not needed simply because an object contains an image.

Keep independent project branding most expressive in Endless. The compact assortment should still belong to the surrounding Smoke & Fireworks direction rather than becoming a row of unrelated advertisements.

Supply meaningful image descriptions, relevant captions and credit. Decorative tape, underlines, flourishes, and purely atmospheric lighting do not need to become screen-reader prose. A chart or diagram needs an understandable textual account of its meaningful result.

Current product sources own facts, releases, links, assets, and disclosure. A composed scene, authored numbers, names, dates and interface contents are finished public content and carry no “illustrative”, “fixture” or “not built” caption (Wolf, 22 October 2026; see Decisions). `illustrative: true` marks unreplaced design-time scaffolding only. Do not include private notes, unused private assets, or withheld paragraphs in a public payload merely because CSS hides them.

## Give Endless rhythm without turning it into a directory

Full width is available composition space, not compulsory text width. One entry may be narrow and off-centre; another can pair an image and paragraph; lineage can cross the frame; a quiet door can interrupt a more active sequence.

Most windows should give one satisfying glimpse, not reproduce the product's manual. A small number of additional lines can make a compact tool understandable. A substantial subject may benefit more from one screenshot than three paragraphs.

Each default is already a usable presentation. Bespoke windows are welcome, not an obligation to design every item from scratch. Each entry may eventually have its own layout, animation, or action without replacing the collection's navigation contract.

Do not introduce a repeated type/title/description/CTA card beneath eight decorative shells. Nor should every subject have a compulsory “Why I made it” section.

## The daily assortment

Aim for roughly five visibly different forms when the real public collection makes that possible. This is a diversity target, not an obligation to show all eight, a fixed number of entries, or permission to manufacture filler.

Variation also comes from size, note variants, material, angle, typography, and the balance between text and imagery. Repeated forms can still produce a good arrangement.

Daily selection and placement belong to the shared host. An ordinary entry author does not write flight trajectories or pack the board manually. Endless order stays curated and stable regardless of which items are selected today.

## Author or improve an entry

1. Establish the real subject, useful public standing, available assets, and appropriate disclosure.
2. Choose its stable ID and physical form. Write the compact encounter as one link to Endless.
3. Choose the useful additional dimension: orientation, paragraph, scribble, image, example, relationship, interaction, links, or deliberate quiet.
4. Use a fitting default window or author a scene: `Compact.astro` and/or `Expanded.astro` under `src/features/pile/entries/<id>/`, found by file, plus `interaction.ts` beside them for behaviour. A compact scene renders inside the object's single link and holds no controls; an expanded scene places exactly one title heading and puts the shared copy block where its composition wants it. Keep navigation, anchors, and mode state with the shared host.
5. Add genuine destinations and credits. Keep unconfirmed claims and private material out of the public entry.
6. Place the entry in the curated Endless order and decide whether it is eligible for the daily Pile.
7. Inspect both views among their neighbours, on a narrow screen, with keyboard access, and with reduced motion.

When revisiting an entry, preserve its ID and useful relationships. Update claims known to be false; do not add a generic staleness disclaimer instead. A newly available link or asset is an ordinary content improvement, not a schema migration.

The [technical plan](pile-technical-plan.md#authoring-files-and-the-entry-contract) gives the concrete file and component procedure.

## What this return supersedes

The selected opening now scatters the compact objects downward out of view and leaves an intentional empty Pile. Endless slowly opens beneath it. Closing reverses that relationship and gathers the same daily assortment.

Every entry has a shareable anchor in Endless. The path-side signpost is the preferred visual experiment for its marker; a small `#` is the complete fallback. Both have identical link semantics.

The reference mockup's compact source link, outbound ticket stub, and rollable die are not the chosen controls. The expanded note must retain its tape. Lineage is not restricted to somebody else's open-source project. Endless does not reroll daily.

The earlier “unfold while leaving the Pile populated” alternative is no longer an equal option. Signpost artwork, exact motion tuning, and a particular single-door/grouped-door composition remain visual choices; they do not reopen the selected behavior.
